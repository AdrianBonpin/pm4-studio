# 03 — 13.56 MHz: Read · Write · Copy · Erase · Memory

**Ticket criterion:** each of the five 13.56 MHz modes at both sizes, including a
decoded readout with the Records table, a raw-only fallback, and the Copy
confirmation. The Write mode's image must show the Draft **list**, not a
dropdown.

**What is fixture and what is not:** the poll replies are the recorded captures
from `docs/PROTOCOL.md` (NTAG215, MIFARE Classic 1K) and one deliberately
unlisted ATQA/SAK pair, all fed in as bytes. The decoding, the layout, the wording
and the guards are the app's. No Reader was attached: every image below was taken
against `e2e.html` with `src/dev/deviceStub.ts` standing at the Tauri invoke
boundary (Vite on port 1421, `bun run dev -- --port 1421`, branch
`ticket/07-e2e-destinations` off integration `d6eadd2`).

## What the decoded readout actually says

From `hf-read-decoded-980.png` and `hf-read-records-980.png`, for the NTAG215
capture:

- UID `04 43 8A 01 22 09 03` — **seven bytes**, decoded, no raw fallback. The
  spec's criterion 3 asks for exactly this on hardware; here it is the decoder
  doing it on recorded bytes.
- Card type **"MIFARE Ultralight / NTAG21x"**, not "NTAG215": ATQA `44 00` with
  SAK `00` is shared by the whole Ultralight/21x family, and the app names the
  range rather than picking a member. That is the honesty rule working. The value
  wraps — two lines at 980, three at 900 — because it is a value in a four-column
  grid, not a control label (see the no-wrap note in
  [06-long-label-sweep.md](06-long-label-sweep.md)).
- Capacity 496 bytes, NFC Forum Type 2, NDEF formatted yes (settled by the memory
  read, not by the family), technologies `ISO/IEC 14443-3A · MIFARE Ultralight`.
- Records: `1 Text hello 12 B yes`, `2 URL https://devgo.studio 17 B yes`, with
  "2 Records in 29 bytes, in the order the Tag holds them."
- *Edit as Draft* sits under the Records rather than in the panel header, with the
  caption "Copies this message into a new Draft, named after the Tag, and opens it
  in Library. The Tag is not written to."
- Raw payload: 19 bytes, `01 01 04 43 8A 01 22 09 03 1D 00 20 07 44 00 00 02 00 00`.

## The raw-only fallback

ATQA `00 00`, SAK `88`, UID 4 bytes — a pair that matches no published
assignment. The readout says **"Not decoded"** with an *unknown card type* badge,
explains that "a wrong family is worse than none", shows the 19 raw bytes
(`01 01 5A 5B 5C 5D 00 00 00 00 00 00 04 00 00 88 01 00 00`), and states "No
message read was attempted, because there is no decoded UID to match one
against." Nothing is offered that needs a decoded UID: no Records row, no *Edit as
Draft*, and *Save to library* stays disabled. The message in the corner for this
read is "The poll reply could not be decoded." with the warning symbol — not a
success.

## The Write mode is a Draft list, not a dropdown

The native `<select>` is gone. What renders in its place, read out of the DOM at
both sizes:

- a `role="group"` whose accessible name (via `aria-labelledby`) is **"Draft to
  write"** — rendered as the small caps label `DRAFT TO WRITE`;
- above it a `role="textbox"` with accessible name **"Search Drafts"** and
  placeholder "Search name, id or content" (shown because there is more than one
  Draft);
- two `button` rows, each with `aria-pressed`, whose labels are
  `Office guest Wi-Fi — 2 Records · 131 bytes · 1d ago` and
  `Desk contact card — 1 Record · 162 bytes · 3d ago`. The em dash and the middle
  dots are the accessible name, not a visual aside;
- on arrival **neither row is pressed** and the write panel is absent. Choosing a
  row sets `aria-pressed="true"` and reveals the *Write to a tag* section, whose
  marker is the **Check tag** button.

The search was driven with real keystrokes in both directions: typing `dev`
narrowed the two rows to `Desk contact card` (that string is inside its contact
email `hello@devgo.studio`, so the match is over authored Record values, not the
name), typing `zzz` replaced the rows with "No Draft matches “zzz”. There are 2
Drafts.", and deleting the three characters brought both rows back in their
original order. One driver note for anyone re-running this: a scripted
*value-set* on the search input does not reach the component's state (React's
change tracker ignores it); the capture used typing and Backspace, which a person
also does.

The relative ages (`1d ago`, `3d ago`) come from fixed `updatedAt` values in the
fixture compared against the capture date, so they will drift if these images are
ever regenerated on another day. Everything else in the row is stable.

## The Write confirmation

`hf-write-confirmation-*` shows the confirmation as it arrives: "Replace the
message on the tag with this Draft?", then "Writing replaces what the tag holds
now: 2 Records, starting with Text hello.", then "The message needs 134 of the
tag's 496 bytes. A write replaces the whole message; nothing is appended.",
then the tag line `tag 04 43 8A 01 22 09 03 · pages 4–37 (34 pages) of user
memory`, then the message hex.

**Observation (new in this pass): the confirmation's own buttons arrive below the
fold.** With the Draft list above it, the Mode's pane needs 850 px of content
against 724 px of viewport at 980×760 and 664 px at 900×700. The **Write to tag**
button's box is y = 792.9…826.9 at both widths — 33 px past the bottom edge at
980×760 and 93 px past it at 900×700 — so confirming a write needs one scroll at
either size. The scrolled pair shows the buttons and the copy that remains above
them. This is the same class as the Erase finding (F3) that was fixed before this
pass; it is recorded here rather than fixed, because this ticket captures and
corrects, and `src/pages/HfPage.tsx` / `src/components/WriteDraftToTag.tsx` are
not in its scope.

## The Copy confirmation

`hf-copy-confirmation-980.png` shows the whole flow on one screen: *Source
message held* — `2 Records, starting with Text hello · 29 bytes`, the source UID
`04 43 8A 01 22 09 03`, and the two Records — then the confirmation "Replace what
tag 04 9A 2B 3C 4D 5E 60 holds?", "Copying writes 2 Records, starting with Text
hello onto this tag, replacing what it holds now: 1 Record: Text old poster, 17
bytes.", the destination's own Record, and "The message needs 32 of this tag's 144
bytes." The fit is 32 because the message is 29 bytes plus the three-byte TLV
wrapper.

At 900×700 the same confirmation needs 724 px of content in a 664 px viewport, so
its own buttons land at y = 692.9…726.9 — 27 px past the bottom edge, one 60 px
scroll away; `hf-copy-confirmation-scrolled-900.png` shows them.

The tag swap between the two reads is the one thing a script cannot click: the
source was removed and the destination presented through the fixture's control
surface (`window.__PM4_DEVICE_FIXTURE_BACKEND__.control.setHf`), which is the same
control the hidden panel exposes for a human. The two buttons either side of it —
*Check the field* and *Read destination tag* — were pressed as themselves.

## Observations raised by this area

- **The Write and Copy confirmations put their own buttons below the fold**
  (measured above: 33/93 px for Write at 980/900, 27 px for Copy at 900). Not a
  defect of the confirmation's copy, which is shorter than Erase's was; it is the
  cost of the always-visible Draft list sitting above the write panel. Recorded,
  not fixed, in this pass.
- **Value wrapping in the Memory panel persists.** At 980 and at 900 the UID
  column wraps `04 43 8A 01 22 09 03` onto a second line, the read timestamp wraps
  mid-token (`2026-11-` / `22T16:13:48.872Z`), and *Pages* wraps `16 of 256 pages`
  / `readable`. Values, not controls: the no-wrap criterion is not violated, but
  it reads as sloppy. Carried into [08-findings.md](08-findings.md) unchanged.
- **At 900×700 the Memory page table scrolls sideways.** The table's wrapper is
  626 px wide with 648 px of content, so its *STATE* column is clipped (the header
  reads `STAT`). The same clipping is visible in the previous capture at this
  size, so it is recorded as unchanged rather than as a regression.
- **F2 — Copy refuses a destination that holds no message** was **not re-run** in
  this pass. The confirmation images above use a destination that already holds a
  message (`ntag-dest`, holding `Text old poster`), which is the only destination
  Copy currently accepts; the empty-destination profile still exists in the
  fixture. The finding stands as written in
  [08-findings.md](08-findings.md) and
  `docs/tickets/studio-redesign-followups/copy-refuses-a-destination-that-holds-no-message.md`;
  this report claims nothing new about it.

## What is *not* established here

- That a MIFARE Classic 1K reads a 4-byte UID on hardware, and that its memory
  dump names no family (hardware checklist, item 8 — **unrun**).
- That a 7-byte NTAG reads without fallback on hardware (item 9 — **unrun**).
- That Copy verifies against a real read-back, or refuses an oversize destination
  before writing (item 10 — **unrun**).
- That this Reader's 13.56 MHz write command does anything at all. The Write and
  Copy confirmations photographed here are confirmations, not results.
- That the Draft list finds a Draft in a library of hundreds: the fixture holds
  two, and the scrolling list, the search and the no-match sentence are what is
  shown.
