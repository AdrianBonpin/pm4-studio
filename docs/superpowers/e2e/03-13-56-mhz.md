# 03 — 13.56 MHz: Read · Write · Copy · Erase · Memory

**Ticket criterion:** each of the five 13.56 MHz modes at both sizes, including a
decoded readout with the Records table, a raw-only fallback, and the Copy
confirmation.

**What is fixture and what is not:** the poll replies are the recorded captures
from `docs/PROTOCOL.md` (NTAG215, MIFARE Classic 1K) and one deliberately
unlisted ATQA/SAK pair, all fed in as bytes. The decoding, the layout, the wording
and the guards are the app's.

## What the decoded readout actually says

From `hf-read-decoded-980.png`, for the NTAG215 capture:

- UID `04 43 8A 01 22 09 03` — **seven bytes**, decoded, no raw fallback. The
  spec's criterion 3 asks for exactly this on hardware; here it is the decoder
  doing it on recorded bytes.
- Card type **"MIFARE Ultralight / NTAG21x"**, not "NTAG215": ATQA `44 00` with
  SAK `00` is shared by the whole Ultralight/21x family, and the app names the
  range rather than picking a member. That is the honesty rule working.
- Capacity 496 bytes, NFC Forum Type 2, NDEF formatted yes (settled by the memory
  read, not by the family).
- Records: `1 Text hello 12 B yes`, `2 URL https://devgo.studio 17 B yes`, with
  "2 Records in 29 bytes, in the order the Tag holds them."

## The raw-only fallback

ATQA `00 00`, SAK `88`, UID 4 bytes — a pair that matches no published
assignment. The readout says **"Not decoded · unknown card type"**, explains that
a wrong family is worse than none, shows the 19 raw bytes, and offers no Records
read because there is no decoded UID to read against. The message in the corner is
the warning symbol, not a success.

## Findings raised by this area

- **F2 — Copy refuses a destination that holds no message.** Copying to a tag that
  is NDEF formatted with an empty NDEF TLV, or not formatted at all, is refused as
  *"the destination's message could not be read, so nothing is replaced"*. An empty
  tag is the ordinary destination for a copy. Follow-up ticket:
  `docs/tickets/studio-redesign-followups/copy-refuses-a-destination-that-holds-no-message.md`.
  Evidence: the refusal was produced live in the browser against the
  `ntag-empty` fixture (NTAG215, empty TLV); the confirmation screenshots above
  use a destination that already holds a message, which is the only destination
  Copy currently accepts.
- **F3 — the Erase confirmation is taller than the window, at both sizes.** Its
  own buttons are below the fold: measured at 900×700 the content column needs
  799 px in a 664 px viewport, and at 980×760 the buttons sit at y=743…777 in a
  760 px window. Confirming an erase means scrolling first. Follow-up ticket:
  `docs/tickets/studio-redesign-followups/the-erase-confirmation-is-taller-than-the-window.md`.
- **Observation — value wrapping in the Memory panel.** At 980 px the UID column
  wraps `04 9A 2B 3C 4D 5E 60` onto a second line and the read timestamp wraps
  mid-token (`2026-11-` / `21T22:56:32.303Z`). Values, not controls: the no-wrap
  criterion is not violated, but it reads as sloppy. Recorded in
  [08-findings.md](08-findings.md).

## What is *not* established here

- That a MIFARE Classic 1K reads a 4-byte UID on hardware, and that its memory
  dump names no family (hardware checklist, item 8 — **unrun**).
- That a 7-byte NTAG reads without fallback on hardware (item 9 — **unrun**).
- That Copy verifies against a real read-back, or refuses an oversize destination
  before writing (item 10 — **unrun**).
- That this Reader's 13.56 MHz write command does anything at all. The Write and
  Copy confirmations photographed here are confirmations, not results.
